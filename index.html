FAQ

Questions, answered

Will the apps be in my company's name?

Yes. The apps are published on Google Play and the App Store under your own developer accounts, with your brand name, logo and colours. We guide you through creating the accounts.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We set everything up and train you and your team. Day to day you only use the admin panel and the store app, which are designed for non-technical users.

How long does it take to launch?

It depends on the package, your branding and app store review times. We'll give you a clear timeline during the consultation.

Which payment methods are supported?

Razorpay for cards, UPI and wallets, plus cash on delivery and an in-app wallet. We help you connect your own Razorpay account so money goes straight to you.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name and logo, Play Store / Apple developer accounts, a Razorpay account, a domain if you have one, and your product list. We help with anything missing.

Can I use it for one kirana store?

Yes. Start with one store and add more stores, delivery zones and riders as you grow.

What happens after launch?

Each package includes support (1, 3 or 6 months). After that you can add the optional maintenance plan from ₹4,999/month for updates, fixes and help.

Are there any other costs?

Prices exclude GST. Third-party costs are paid by you directly: domain, hosting/VPS, Google Play account (US$25 one-time), Apple Developer Program (US$99/year, Premium only), SMS/OTP and payment-gateway fees.

Can I see it before deciding?

Yes — try the live demo, or ask for a guided walkthrough on a call.
