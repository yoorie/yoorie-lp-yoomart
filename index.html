FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? WhatsApp us or email support@yoorie.com.

Will the apps be under my brand and my account?

Yes. The apps carry your name, logo and colours, and on Business and Premium they're published on your own Google Play (and Apple) developer account.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. We do the setup, and train you and your staff to run orders, products and riders from simple apps and the admin panel.

How long does it take to launch?

Usually a few weeks. It depends on the package, how quickly you approve branding and send product data, and app-store review times.

Are there any other costs?

Yes, third-party costs are paid directly by you: domain, hosting/VPS, Google Play ($25 one-time), Apple Developer ($99/year, for iOS), SMS/OTP and payment-gateway fees. Package prices exclude GST.

Do you take a commission on my orders?

No. You pay a one-time setup fee and, if you want it, an optional monthly maintenance plan.

Can I see it working before I decide?

Yes. Try the live demo, or ask for a walkthrough on the free call.

Can it work for more than one store?

Yes. The platform supports multiple stores, so you can start with one and add more later.
